Insight Work

Survey Research & Segmentation

Reaching the audiences no survey had found before

Tradu / Stratos Markets — 2024 to 2026
+5% Conversion rate improvement attributed to insight-led roadmap changes
3+ Distinct barrier profiles identified across disengaged segments

Standard survey panels kept returning the same engaged users. The disengaged customers — the ones who had registered but never converted, or who had converted and gone quiet — were invisible to conventional approaches.

Designed a targeted survey programme from scratch that reached those specific segments through alternative recruitment channels, adapted question design for low-engagement respondents and ran the analysis to identify distinct barrier profiles that did not exist in the existing data.

The findings changed product roadmap priorities and fed directly into a 5% improvement in conversion rate. The programme became a standing component of the quarterly insight calendar.

Longitudinal Mixed-Methods Programme

Understanding product adoption over time, not just at a moment

Tradu / Stratos Markets — 2024 to 2026
Multiple Product investment decisions influenced by longitudinal findings
Executive Level presentation of findings to C-suite and board

Point-in-time research kept answering the wrong question. What the product team needed was not how customers felt on day one, but how their relationship with the product changed over weeks and months of use.

Built and ran a longitudinal programme combining diary studies, repeat qualitative interviews and quantitative tracking to follow the same cohorts over time. Surfaced adoption barriers and behavioural patterns that cross-sectional methods had been missing entirely.

Findings were presented at executive level and directly influenced priorities and investment decisions across product and commercial functions.

Insight Infrastructure Build

Building a research function where there was none

FXCM — 2021 to 2023
From 0 Research function built, governed and scaled
Full stack Qual, quant, analytics and experimentation running concurrently

Joined a marketing team with no research practice, no consent frameworks, no tooling and no established methods for understanding customers. Left it with a fully operational mixed-methods function that influenced product, marketing and commercial decisions.

Introduced structured research practices, built ethical consent frameworks and governance standards compliant with GDPR, onboarded a tool ecosystem in phases and ran qualitative and quantitative programmes simultaneously across multiple markets and customer segments.

The function scaled from one person doing informal research to a VP-level capability sitting at the executive table.
