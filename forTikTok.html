Profile

Research built for environments that don't slow down.

The research function did not exist when I arrived. Building it meant starting with the question every sceptical organisation asks first: why do we need this? Answering that question is not a research problem. It is a trust problem. The only way through it is to produce evidence that changes decisions, repeatedly, until research stops being something the organisation tolerates and starts being something it depends on. That is what happened at FXCM. Then I did it again at Tradu.

Owned the full function: qualitative and quantitative, experimentation, behavioural analytics, VoC. Every method connected to every other, feeding into a single picture of the customer that the whole organisation could act on. Not a research service sitting to the side of the product. A research function at the centre of how decisions got made.

The VoC programme brought all of those channels together and made that picture visible company-wide. Different teams, different stakeholders, one shared view. That is what turns research from a cost into an asset.

Homepage and Landing Page Optimisation + Data Review + Qual Research + A/B Testing
Research-led optimisation programme: from data to hypothesis to measurable results
75%→50%Bounce rate
3.17%→9%CTA conversion

The product had extensive analytics and an active experimentation programme. What it lacked was the qualitative layer that could explain why users were behaving as the data described. The research brief was to close that gap: take the data, understand the mechanisms behind the patterns, generate hypotheses, and feed them into the experimentation cycle.

Started with a behavioural data review to identify where the highest-impact friction lived. Ran qualitative research with users to understand what was driving the patterns. Translated findings into prioritised hypotheses that the experimentation team could test. Research contributed to scroll depth improving from 15% to 31%, bounce rate falling from 75% to 50%, CTA conversion rising from 3.17% to 9%, journey completion up 24%, and overall conversion up 5%.

Longitudinal Qualitative Research + Diary Studies + In-Depth Interviews
Surfacing what standard testing could not in a complex trading platform beta
BetaInsights standard testing could not reach

Beta testing and product analytics were already running. What they could not capture was how users developed, or failed to develop, confidence and competence with a complex financial product over time. A single session could not show that. Longitudinal research could.

Designed and ran a qualitative longitudinal programme on the trading platform in beta, tracking the same cohort through diary studies and repeat in-depth interviews across multiple months. The research surfaced blockers and friction patterns that neither usability testing nor product data had identified, specific moments where the product's mental model diverged from the user's, and where that divergence compounded into disengagement. The findings provided multiple actionable items that contributed to improved retention and engagement.

Survey Programme + NPS + Journey Optimisation
Scaling a survey programme to reach hard-to-reach audiences and drive journey improvements
-4%Onboarding abandonment rate

Standard survey deployment reaches the customers who are already engaged. The customers generating the most friction, abandoning journeys and driving contact volumes are the least likely to respond. Closing that gap required rethinking how and where surveys were served, not just what they asked.

Managed and evolved the survey programme across FXCM and Tradu. Integrated rating scales directly into email campaigns and FAQ articles on the site, creating a continuous NPS signal that served both product improvement and regulatory reporting purposes. Updated survey journeys to target high-abandonment flows specifically, reaching segments that had previously been invisible to research. Findings from the programme fed directly into journey redesign work. Onboarding abandonment rate improved by 4%. The programme also provided a consistent, auditable insight layer that satisfied regulatory visibility requirements.

Research Function Build + Mixed Methods + Capability Development
Building a research function that runs across qualitative, quantitative and experimental methods
x2Research functions built from scratch

At both FXCM and Tradu, the research function did not exist before I arrived. At FXCM I introduced research into an organisation that had no existing practice and built the methods, processes and stakeholder buy-in from the ground up. At Tradu I did it again, faster and with higher complexity — sole researcher owning the full agenda across qual, quant and experimental research simultaneously.

At Tradu, built the full infrastructure: ethical frameworks, participant consent processes, data governance standards, research repositories and tool ecosystems. Established coaching programmes for product managers and designers in interviewing technique, bias mitigation and ethical data handling. Developed playbooks and office-hours processes giving teams a path to run their own research with rigour. Set quality standards and guardrails for AI-assisted research methods. The result was a research function that generated insight across more teams more frequently, without becoming the sole gatekeeper for every study.
