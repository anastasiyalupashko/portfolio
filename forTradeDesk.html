About

Research as a way of thinking, not just a job.

Started in a client-facing financial services role with no research function around her, introduced one anyway and never stopped building from there. What began as curiosity about why customers were struggling with complex products turned into five years of mixed-methods practice, two research functions built from scratch and a VP appointment before turning 30.

The belief at the centre of all of it: research is only worth doing if it changes what gets built. That means fighting for access to the right users, being honest when the data contradicts a decision already made, and finding the framing that makes evidence land with people who were not looking for it.

Outside work: competed as a rhythmic and aesthetic gymnast at European and World Championship level for fifteen years. Supports the Immune Deficiency Foundation, Walk for Home and the Alzheimer's Society Memory Walk.
